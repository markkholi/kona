Kona Book Recommender

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Kona ("we", "our", or "the app") is a youth book recommendation mobile application designed for school kids aged 10 to 17, educators, parents, and librarians. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration, login credentials, or personal identity information required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or GPS location data collected.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data in any manner.

2. On-Device Storage

Any reader age selected, interest queries entered, bookmarked reading lists, and recent searches remain stored exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage (AsyncStorage). If you provide a Google Gemini or OpenAI API key in Settings, it is stored locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

3. External Services

  • Google Books API: Used solely to fetch book covers, publication dates, and ISBN numbers. No personal identifiers are transmitted.
  • AI Recommendation Engine (Optional): If you configure a custom API key, prompts containing the target age and interest topic are sent directly to the respective API provider.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Kona complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect personal information from children under 13.

# Privacy Policy for Kona

**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

**Kona** ("we", "our", or "the app") is a youth book recommendation mobile application designed for school kids aged 10 to 17, educators, parents, and librarians. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal identity information.
- We do not collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.
- We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or tracking cookies.
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data in any manner.

### 2. On-Device Storage
- Any interests typed by the user, selected reader ages, saved book lists (reading bookmarks), and recent search queries are stored locally on the user's device using standard sandboxed storage (`AsyncStorage`).
- If you optionally provide an API key (e.g. Google Gemini or OpenAI) in the Settings screen, that key is saved only in sandboxed storage on your device and is never sent to our servers.
- You can clear your search history and saved reading lists at any time within the app's Settings screen or by clearing the app data.

### 3. External Services and Network Requests
- **Google Books API:** When looking up publication details, book cover thumbnails, and ISBN numbers, the app queries the public Google Books API using the title and author of the recommended books. No personal user information is transmitted during these queries.
- **AI Recommendation Engine (Optional):** If you choose to configure a personal Google Gemini or OpenAI API key, prompts containing the target age (e.g., 12) and requested interest (e.g., "space exploration") are sent directly to that provider's API. No personal identifiers are included in these requests.

### 4. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
Kona complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All data entered into the app remains strictly on the user's device.
